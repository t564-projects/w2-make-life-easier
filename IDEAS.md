## Idea: Fresh Restock Assistant

Pulls my Amazon Fresh order history, learns what I buy, how much and how often, and emails me when an item is due. It can then fill my Fresh cart (Alexa skill or browser automation) so I only review and check out. A local dashboard shows the logic behind each prediction and lets me edit it. Full design in [design-grocery-shopping.md](design-grocery-shopping.md).
